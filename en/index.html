The counter

Recipes from the Baltics and France, baked in our own kitchen.

Every piece carries a stamp, like the maker’s mark under a porcelain plate: where the recipe comes from. The counter changes daily, so if you want something for sure, order it.

Most of our products contain allergens (gluten, dairy, egg, nuts…). Please ask the team before ordering.

No two cups alike.

We serve in vintage porcelain (English, Bavarian, French), with roses and gilded rims. Every table gets different pieces, so tomorrow’s coffee won’t look like today’s.

Moraira · 4 pm to 10 pm

When Dénia closes, the lights come on in Moraira.

Our second home, on Av. de la Paz in Moraira, also opens in the evening from Wednesday to Sunday. Almost everything is made from scratch: slow-cooked ribs, duck, tuna carpaccio with chilli, tartare, homemade pâté, Russian salad with prawns… and a good list of natural wines.
